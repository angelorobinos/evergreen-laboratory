## Research

I am currently working on my doctoral research on **lignin-derived hard carbon materials for sodium-ion batteries**.

Much of my time is spent investigating how metal–lignin interactions influence carbon structure formation during pyrolysis. Recent work has focused on magnesium–lignin coordination, porosity development, and the resulting electrochemical behavior of the carbon materials.

Alongside experimental work, I am also running computational studies to better understand the binding interactions between magnesium ions and lignin model structures.
