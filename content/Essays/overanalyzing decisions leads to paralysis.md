Everyday, we are forced to make countless decisions. But not all of the stakes are equal, some are small decisions, some can affect us moderately, and a few are life-changing ones.

One should not overanalyze small decisions. Decide quickly if there are no massive repercussions. Spend a little bit of time for those that can affect us for a period of time. But for life changing decisions, spend a bit more time.

However, be careful not to get paralyzed by the decision-making. Life is full of mistakes, we might make a mistake in the decisions, but it is also a process of learning. 

What is worse if we dont make any decisions at all, thus making us fall into the trap of analysis paralysis.
